# SOP-002 Product Quality Complaint Triage

> FICTIONAL training document for the Pharma Safety Intake & Triage Tracker prototype. Not a real procedure.

## Purpose
This procedure explains how product quality complaints are captured and organised for the quality assurance team. A product quality complaint describes a possible defect in a product, its packaging or its labelling, without a reported adverse event. If the reporter also describes an adverse event, a separate Suspected Adverse Event report is created as well.

## Defect categories
Operators describe the defect in neutral, factual language using one of these categories: physical appearance (discoloured, cracked, chipped or broken tablets, capsules that are split or empty), contamination (foreign particles, visible specks, unusual odour), packaging integrity (broken blister seals, leaking bottles, damaged vials, missing tamper-evident seals), labelling (missing, misprinted or illegible labels, wrong leaflet) and device function (pumps or droppers that fail to dispense).

## Batch and lot traceability
The batch or lot number links a complaint to manufacturing records. Always copy the lot exactly as printed on the pack, in the format LOT-NNNNN. The knowledge assistant can search past reports by lot number, for example a query for a specific lot such as LOT-88392 lists every complaint and report recorded against that batch. Several complaints against the same lot are highlighted to the quality team. The tracker itself makes no quality release or recall decision.

## Complaint samples
When a reporter still has the affected pack, the operator records whether a sample is available for return. The operator does not ask the reporter to send medicine by post without the returns kit described by the quality team. Sample availability is recorded in the description field in neutral wording.

## Escalation to quality assurance
After intake, the complaint is moved to In Review and the quality assurance team is notified through the normal team queue. Investigation outcomes are recorded in the quality system, not in this prototype. When the quality team confirms the intake record is complete, the report can be put forward for closure, which requires reviewer approval as described in SOP-004.
